# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Faultline is intended to be a professional, technically rigorous, and welcoming open-source project. Everyone participating in the project is expected to treat other contributors and users with respect, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive community include:

- discussing technical disagreements on their merits;
- giving specific, constructive feedback;
- accepting corrections when evidence shows a claim is wrong;
- respecting privacy and avoiding disclosure of credentials, customer data, or other sensitive information;
- clearly distinguishing measured evidence, simulation, inference, and opinion;
- being patient with contributors who are learning the codebase or networking concepts;
- focusing criticism on code, design, evidence, and behaviour rather than on people.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks;
- personal attacks or deliberately inflammatory behaviour;
- sexualised language or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, reviews, or discussions;
- knowingly submitting malicious code, credentials, private customer information, or fabricated evidence as genuine measurement;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional technical community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is publicly representing the Faultline project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the project owner through an appropriate private GitHub channel where one is available. Do not publish sensitive personal information in a public issue.

If a report concerns the project owner, use GitHub's platform reporting or support mechanisms rather than sending the report to the project owner.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity and history, enforcement may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project participation.

Reports will be considered in good faith and handled with as much confidentiality as the circumstances allow.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is informed by the principles of the Contributor Covenant and adapted for the Faultline project.
